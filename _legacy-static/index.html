01Philosophy

We measure success in decades, not quarters, and invest only where we can add lasting value.

HAGER Investment was founded on a simple conviction: capital is most productive when it is patient, considered and closely held. We act as long-term stewards of the assets we own, working alongside management teams and partners to strengthen them over full economic cycles.

Our independence allows us to decide on merit alone. We are free of short-term pressure, we follow a selective investment process, and we answer directly to the families, foundations and institutions whose capital we look after.

  1. I

    Stewardship

    We hold assets as an owner would. We invest in their quality, their people and their resilience.

  2. II

    Discipline

    Rigorous underwriting, conservative leverage and a willingness to walk away when the price is wrong.

  3. III

    Alignment

    Our principals invest alongside our partners in every strategy, on identical terms.

02Strategies

Four strategies, one long-term discipline.

Each strategy is led by a dedicated team with deep sector expertise. All of them share the same governance, risk framework and commitment to responsible ownership.

  1. Core and value-add residential, logistics and office assets in Europe's most resilient metropolitan markets. We focus on long-term income and active asset management.

    • Residential
    • Logistics
    • Prime office

  2. Majority and significant minority stakes in established, family-owned and founder-led businesses in the DACH region and the Benelux. We back them with patient capital and practical operational support.

    • Mid-market
    • Succession
    • Growth

  3. Essential infrastructure and energy transition assets, from renewable generation to digital networks, with contracted, inflation-linked cash flows and measurable environmental impact.

    • Renewables
    • Digital
    • Transport

  4. Senior and unitranche financing for high-quality European companies and real assets. We emphasise capital preservation, strong covenants and direct relationships with borrowers.

    • Senior secured
    • Unitranche
    • Real estate debt

03Approach

A considered process, from first conversation to final exit.

Every investment follows the same four stages, overseen by an independent investment committee and supported by a dedicated risk function.

  1. Stage 01

    Origination

    Proprietary sourcing through long-standing relationships with families, entrepreneurs and advisers across Europe.

  2. Stage 02

    Underwriting

    Independent due diligence covering commercial, financial, legal and ESG factors, stress-tested against adverse scenarios.

  3. Stage 03

    Ownership

    Active stewardship and a clear value-creation plan, with governance designed for transparency and accountability.

  4. Stage 04

    Realisation

    Exits timed to the asset rather than the calendar. Where it makes sense, we hold indefinitely.
